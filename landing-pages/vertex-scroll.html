The camera doesn't cut. It commits.

How it's built

Three ingredients, one smooth ride.

No engine, no build step — just Three.js reading your scroll position every frame and translating it into a camera path, with GSAP handling the words that ride alongside it.

Scroll-synced camera

A Catmull-Rom curve through every checkpoint, sampled by scroll progress and eased with a frame-rate-independent lerp — smooth on a trackpad or a scroll wheel.

GPU-light objects

Four primitives, standard materials, one directional light and a rim light — the whole scene stays under budget on a mid-range laptop or a phone.

Plug-and-play sections

Every text block is a plain HTML section with a scroll checkpoint id. Add one, add a matching camera keyframe, done — no re-authoring the scene.
